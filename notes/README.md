# Notes

Research done while building the sign. These are working notes, kept for reference. The [build guide](../docs/guide.md) is the place to start.

- [display-st7796.md](display-st7796.md): the 3.5" ST7796S display module, its datasheet, the vendor init sequence, and how `mipidsi` drives it from the ESP32.
- [font-measurements-a.md](font-measurements-a.md): the first study of the classic sign's fonts and layout, measured LED by LED from station photos.
- [font-measurements-b.md](font-measurements-b.md): a second, independent study of the same fonts and layout, used to cross-check the first.
- [power.md](power.md): what the sign draws on USB, the firmware changes that cut it, and what a battery version would take.
- [sourcing.md](sourcing.md): display and ESP32 board options and prices for building 10 to 50 signs.
